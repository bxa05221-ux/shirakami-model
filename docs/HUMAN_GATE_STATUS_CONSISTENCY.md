# Human Gate Status Consistency

The repository treats Human Gate status as a controlled state, not free-form documentation.

The `Human Gate Status Consistency` workflow checks that the authoritative decision record, Canonical Model, invariant coverage matrix, and status contract agree on the current decision state.

## Invariant

A technical PASS may establish evidence, but it cannot establish Human Gate approval.

Likewise, once Human Gate approval is explicitly recorded, normative documents must not continue to claim that the decision is pending.

The consistency workflow is a guard against stale status, not a substitute for Human Gate authority.
