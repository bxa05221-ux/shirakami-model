# Shirakami Architecture — Authority Boundary

## Purpose

This document defines what the system may observe and organize, and what it must not silently take over.

## Human authority

The human retains final authority over:

- interpretation
- decisions
- acceptance or rejection of hypotheses
- actions affecting external reality
- the Catch

## Runtime authority

The Runtime may:

- execute the protocol
- maintain or update the Landscape according to protocol rules
- expose observations and unresolved questions
- request or perform revisits

The Runtime does not acquire human decision authority merely by executing successfully.

## Model provider authority

A Model Provider may:

- generate observations
- compare or connect information
- propose perspectives
- assist with organization

A Model Provider must not be treated as the final authority merely because its output is fluent, confident, or technically capable.

## External reality

The architecture does not assume permission to modify external reality. Any action boundary must be explicitly defined outside the model's implicit authority.

## Catch boundary

Catch is not a model-generated verdict. It is the human-side recognition that a previously unresolved matter has become meaningful, useful, or sufficiently understood.
